# Founder Story: {First Name}

> **What this is.** The founder's own story and voice, kept close to verbatim from the
> voice notes of {date}. Quotable lines may appear in content as first-person founder
> quotes. When writing in their name, mirror this phrasing.

## Why they started

{The founding story in their words. Keep the specifics: the year, the job, the moment,
what they wanted to be before this.}

## What they refuse to do

{The refusals, verbatim where possible. This is usually where the brand's wedge lives.}

## Their values

{The two or three things they won't compromise on, as they phrased them.}

## Customers they remember

{1 to 3 short customer stories they retold. Real details, first person preserved.}

## Life outside the work

{Family, hobbies, the good Saturday. One short paragraph; humanizes local content.}

## The ten-year answer

{What they want people in their town to say about them and the business.}

## How they talk (voice samples)

{5 to 10 verbatim lines worth quoting: phrases they repeat, how they explain the trade,
what they say when a customer thanks them. These calibrate every piece written in their
voice.}
