# Prohibited Claims: {Business Name}

> **What this is.** Things that must never appear in content written in this client's name.
> Starts with the generic list (applies to almost any business); add client-specific entries
> from the interview (things they refused, unconfirmed third-party claims, regulated
> territory in their trade).

## Generic starter list (keep unless the client has proof on file)

1. **Superlatives without third-party proof**: best, greatest, #1, top-rated, most trusted,
   industry-leading, world-class.
2. **Open-ended promises**: lifetime warranty/guarantee, permanent fix, forever.
3. **Absolute safety/effectiveness**: 100% safe, completely safe, risk-free, guaranteed
   results.
4. **Exclusivity without verification**: first, only, the only one who.
5. **Price-leadership claims**: cheapest, lowest price guaranteed. (State real prices or
   approved savings ranges instead.)
6. **Named-competitor comparisons**: "better than {competitor}", "unlike {competitor}".
   Competitors may appear only as the buyer's prior awareness, never as a comparison target.
7. **Unapproved credential implications**: certified/licensed/insured beyond exactly what
   `approved-claims.md` documents. Especially careful in regulated trades (childcare,
   health, finance, anything touching safety).
8. **Invented social proof**: composite customer quotes, invented statistics.
