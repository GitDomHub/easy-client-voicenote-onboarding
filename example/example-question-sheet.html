Your story~3 min
  1. Your site says over 20 years of experience. What got you started, and was there a moment you decided this was the business?
  2. What do other window cleaning outfits around Maplewood do that you refuse to do?
  3. Tell me about one customer or job you still remember. What happened?
  4. When a customer thanks you, what do they usually say you did differently?
The person behind the business~3 min
  1. Be honest: window cleaning probably wasn't the childhood dream. What was the plan before this, and how did this become the thing you built your life around?
  2. What are your values? The two or three things you won't compromise on, in business or in life?
  3. Outside the work: family, hobbies, what does a good Saturday look like when you're not up a ladder?
  4. Ten years from now, what do you want people in Maplewood to say about you and ClearView?
The services~2 min
  1. Your site lists 6 services (window cleaning inside and out, eavestrough cleaning, pressure washing, house washing, solar panel cleaning, seasonal property checks). Which ONE do you most want more of?
  2. “Seasonal property checks” is the broadest one on your site. What exactly does it include, and do you offer any packages or services that aren't on the site yet?
The claims (we only publish what you confirm)~2 min
  1. Your site says “fully insured.” What are we allowed to publish beyond that? Coverage amount, anything else a homeowner or property manager would want to see?
  2. Your site says “we stand behind every job.” What is the guarantee exactly, word for word, and what are its conditions?
  3. Your site shows “4.9 stars from 40+ Google reviews.” Is that current, and are you happy for us to always quote the live number as it grows?
How you win work~2 min
  1. What's the most common question people ask when they first call, word for word? And what do you say back?
  2. Think of the moment right before someone says yes. What's the last thing they usually say or ask? That final little doubt?
  3. What do people get wrong about what a job costs? What does the price really depend on that homeowners never think of?
Twenty years of knowing things others don't~3 min
  1. Walk me through your year: when does the phone go crazy, when is it quiet, and what do you wish people booked earlier than they do?
  2. Your site mentions a water-fed pole system that reaches third-storey windows from the ground. What made you invest in that gear, and what can you do now that most outfits around Maplewood can't?
  3. After 20 years: what can you tell about a house in the first two minutes of pulling up that the homeowner has no idea about?
Working together~2 min
  1. Text, call, or email? When's the best time to reach you, and how often do you actually want updates: weekly note, monthly summary, only when something needs you?
  2. When we write something in your name, do you want to read every word before it goes live, or approve the first few and then trust us with spot-checks?
  3. What has a supplier or vendor done in the past that drove you nuts? Tell us so we can do the opposite.
The customers & the area~4 min
  1. Who usually calls you, and is the person who calls the same person who pays? What are they worried about before they call?
  2. When you lose a job to someone else, who wins it and why?
  3. Which customers are the easiest? The jobs that go smooth, pay on time, no drama. What do those people have in common?
  4. And your whales: the biggest jobs or most valuable regulars you have. What are they, and what makes them worth so much?
  5. If we could fill your calendar with just one kind of customer, who would it be? Describe them like you're describing a real person.
  6. Your site lists Maplewood, Cedar Falls, and Bruxton. Where do you WANT more work, and anywhere you'd rather not go?
